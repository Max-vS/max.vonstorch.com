This site is built from one rule: everything sits on a grid of square tiles. Each page picks its own palette and three patterns, and scrolling swaps one pattern for the next.

## Why tiles

A tile is small enough to animate cheaply and big enough to carry a shape. Repeat it and you get texture; rotate one and you get motion. The whole visual language comes from about forty shapes drawn in a 120-unit box.

## How a pattern works

A pattern is just a function from a grid position to a tile:

```ts
type Tile = { shape: string; rotation: number; bg: string; fg: string }

const arcs = (row: number, col: number): Tile =>
  ({ shape: 'qring', rotation: pick([0, 90, 180, 270]), bg: SAGE, fg: FOREST })
```

- The grid size is computed from the screen.
- The title box and card are placed in whole tiles.
- Patterns never know which page they are on.

> Constraints are the fastest way to make something feel designed.
